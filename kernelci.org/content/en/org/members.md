The success of the KernelCI project is driven by contributions from our
developer community and organizations joining as members.

## Why Join?

Every organization invests in kernel validation—finding and fixing bugs before deployment. KernelCI shifts that burden upstream. By establishing testing standards the kernel community adopts, regressions are caught earlier, reducing your downstream validation costs and accelerating time-to-deployment. Membership isn't new spending—it's strategic cost avoidance through pooled testing infrastructure.

### For Cloud Providers

- **Reduce Validation Costs**: Fewer regressions reaching your testing pipelines means less triage and remediation work
- **Accelerate Deployment Cycles**: Higher upstream quality means faster kernel qualification and feature rollout
- **Shape Ecosystem Standards**: Influence which testing practices the kernel community adopts through board governance

### For Hardware Vendors

- **Lower Enablement Costs**: Systematic community testing of ARM, RISC-V, and specialized platforms reduces your support burden
- **Reduce Customer Impact**: Better upstream kernel quality means fewer issues your customers encounter
- **Drive Testing Coverage**: Ensure critical subsystems (drivers, power management, peripherals) are part of standard testing practices

### Universal Benefits

- **Strategic Board Influence**: Shape testing priorities and roadmap through Advisory Board governance
- **Integration Support**: White-glove technical integration of your infrastructure with experienced service providers
- **Thought Leadership**: Position your organization as driving systematic quality improvement in the Linux ecosystem
- **Consolidated Effort**: Move away from fragmented, duplicated testing—share the burden and benefits across the industry

## The Model: Pooled Testing Infrastructure

Think of it like insurance. You can't prove which specific regression your membership prevented, but systematic upstream testing catches bugs that would otherwise reach you downstream. The ecosystem cost to maintain this infrastructure is shared—your membership ensures the system stays viable while reducing individual validation burden.

## The Impact

KernelCI caught hundreds of regressions before they reached stable releases last year, preventing costly downstream validation failures. See the full impact and testing coverage on our [public dashboard](https://dashboard.kernelci.org).
